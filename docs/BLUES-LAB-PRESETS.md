# Blues Lab presets

For the current bank and controls, see the [BluesBox V48 Preset Guide](BluesBox-V48-Preset-Guide.pdf). The original V43 recipes below document the earlier comparison bank.

The experimental build starts with 40 blues presets and keeps the 52-slot preset format. Slots 41–43 carry three V40 references. Slots 44–52 are comparisons. Existing saved presets take precedence over factory defaults. On a device with saved sounds, export them first and use the editor’s **Factory** button for the slots you want to audition.

The 40 song presets share this order when the effects are enabled.

`Gate → Drive → Amp → Cab → Compressor → Modulation → Delay → Reverb`

The post-cab compressor lets the guitar volume change the amount of breakup at the drive and amp. The compressor then acts on the resulting level. Pitch, SFX, and EQ are bypassed in these presets. The three V40 references retain their original routing.

The artist rigs below are listening references. Blues Lab shares four voices drawn from the existing V40 amp and cab library.

| Voice | OpenPocket starting point | What to listen for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blackface | TWIN A2 Lite and 1×12 US OPEN | Clean pick attack and open top end |
| Full | SUPER 59 A2 Lite and 1×12 CREAM | More midrange and a fuller edge-of-breakup sound |
| Headroom | SHIRL CL A2 Lite and 2×12 BLUE | A rounder clean and mild breakup |
| Dry | TWIN A2 Lite and brighter 1×12 US OPEN | A close, drier sound for the King bank |

These voices approximate the broad tonal roles of the original equipment. In particular, the headroom voice is a stand-in for Dumble/Two-Rock-type sounds, and the dry voice is a Fender-based stand-in for King's stage solid-state sound. Cabinet models are the built-in simulations. The presets require no user NAM captures or IRs.

| Slot | Display | What to compare |
| ---: | --- | --- |

| 44 | AB OPTO OLD | Original opto after amp and cab |
| 45 | AB OPTO T4 | T4-inspired opto after the same amp and cab, with matching control settings |
| 46 | AB TS9 V40 | V40 capture-fitted TS9 |
| 47 | AB TS808 LAB | Circuit-inspired 808 experiment |
| 48 | AB TS808 OLD | Legacy OpenPocket TS 808 |
| 49 | AB KLON | V40 capture-fitted Klon |
| 50 | AB BREAKER | Legacy OpenPocket Bluesbreaker |
| 51 | AB CLEAN BST | Circuit-inspired clean boost experiment |
| 52 | AB NO DRIVE | Same drive comparison rig with drive bypassed |

Use slots 2, 21, and 31 for the first artist listening pass. Slots 46–52 share the same amp/cab rig with the time effects bypassed. Slots 44–45 share identical compressor control settings. Their control laws can yield different gain reduction. The comparison presets use separate preset level trims to bring the average loudness close together. Use a repeatable DI performance to compare feel and tone.

Both builds use the same preset data. The 4× build increases drive oversampling before a NAM amp while the NAM capture still runs at its native rate. The 40 song presets averaged about −23 LUFS across the bundled riff, lead, chord, and arpeggio DI loops at the engine’s nominal test input gain. Their highest measured sample peak was −3.1 dBFS before master volume. The three V40 references keep their original levels. TS BLUES was the hottest and reached +0.9 dBFS in that raw host render.

In the private V45 and V46 builds, P21 Gravity uses **TWO-ROCK TS1** with **OPTO T4**, and P22 Slow Dance uses those with **TS808 JAPAN** in the drive slot. The Two-Rock is an AMP type alongside NAM.

## V47 factory variations

The local V47 build uses the fitted **Twin Clean / JBL 2×12**, **Super Clean or Super Edge / CTS 4×10**, **Deluxe Edge / Jensen P12Q**, and **Blonde Bass / Jensen 2×12** voices. Factory P21 and P22 keep **Two-Rock TS1**, paired with the JBL cab. P23 Vultures uses **Twin Edge**. Slots 48–52 start as **USER1–USER5**, with a clean amp and cab ready to edit. The public V47 build uses the available public voices. Both builds keep the 52-slot layout and honour saved presets.


## V48 factory variations

The 40 artist slots keep their song references. The local fitted voices
received targeted presence and cabinet adjustments. P25 uses a
touch-sensitive band-pass effect to approximate the recorded
AdrenaLinn sound. P41–P43 preserve the V40 rigs with new output trims.
P44–P47 hold the opto and drive comparisons. P48–P52 start as USER1–USER5.
All 52 factory output trims were measured on riff, lead, chords, and
arpeggio DI, with two additional comparison clips. Each build uses its
own trims to match its amp and cab models.

- The SRV presets use the Fender side of the Vibroverb/Dumble blend as
  their tonal reference. [Richard Mullen on the Texas Flood sessions](https://www.mixonline.com/recording/classic-tracks/classic-track-stevie-ray-vaughan-pride-and-joy-367582)
- The local Two-Rock TS1 covers one component of Mayer's multi-amp live sound.
  P25 offers a guitar-driven filter sound. Linn documents a separate pulsing
  filter setting for the opening chords and a different setting for the melody.
  [Chad Franscoviak on Mayer's rig](https://www.mixonline.com/live-sound/tour-profile-john-mayer-367966), [Roger Linn's AdrenaLinn II guide](https://www.rogerlinndesign.com/support/support-adrenalinn-2-faqs)

  King's own account describes Acoustic and Roland stage amps.
